Tariff impact calculator

Interactive policy analysis tool, HTML and JavaScript

Shows who bears the cost of a tariff. Enter an imported product's price, the tariff rate, and how much of the tariff the importer passes on to consumers. The tool splits the cost between consumers and the importer and estimates total tariff revenue collected by the government.

How it works

  • Tariff per unit = import price × tariff rate. This is an ad valorem tariff, the most common type.
  • The pass-through rate sets the share of the tariff charged to consumers through higher prices. The importer absorbs the rest through lower margins.
  • Revenue assumes import volume stays the same. In practice, higher prices usually reduce imports, so actual revenue would be lower.
